# What the assistant says. Format: key|text   (edit freely, then ./make_voice.sh && ./install.sh <tv-ip>)
#
# app.<package>  : said when that app's Featured card stays selected
# input.<kind>   : said when you switch input (hdmi, av, tv, usb)
# idle.<package> : said after 15 s without a button press; OK on the assistant card opens that app
# sys.*          : other moments
#
# Record your own audio as voice/recorded/<key>.mp3 (or .wav/.m4a), or use a macOS voice:
#   VOICE=Daniel ./make_voice.sh
